## 1. EAGLE-3 paper (arXiv 2503.01840, https://arxiv.org/html/2503.01840)

- Architecture [V], Sec 3.1/3.2: low, middle, high-level target features (l, m, h) are concatenated (3k dims) and passed through an FC layer to k dims (k = target hidden size). The result is concatenated with the token embedding, passed through another FC to k, then fed to ONE decoder layer. Draft layer size not stated in the text I got. Third-party implementation (below, MLX discussion 890) taps layers {2, N//2, N-3} (so 2, 16, 29 for a 32-layer model) and the head is ~850 MB in PyTorch for Llama-3.1-8B (includes 32k draft vocab and 2x-hidden-size QKV input).
- Training data [V], Sec 4: ShareGPT (~68K entries) + UltraChat-200K (~464K entries). Target model REGENERATES responses ("We call the target model to generate responses rather than using a fixed dataset"). ~8x more data than EAGLE (Sec 1). R1-distill-8B also used OpenThoughts-114k-math.
- Data scaling [V text, no values]: Fig 1 (MT-bench, Llama-3.1-8B) shows speedup rising with data scale relative to ShareGPT.
- Training compute: NOT reported in the paper text I could read [V absence; could be in an appendix I did not see].
- Table 1, Llama-3.1-Instruct 8B, temperature 0, speedup / acceptance length tau [V]:
  - EAGLE-2: MT-bench 3.16x/4.05, HumanEval 3.66x/4.71, GSM8K 3.39x/4.24, Alpaca 3.28x/4.12, CNN/DM 2.65x/3.45, mean 3.23x/4.11.
  - EAGLE-3: MT-bench 4.40x/6.13, HumanEval 4.85x/6.74, GSM8K 4.48x/6.23, Alpaca 4.82x/6.70, CNN/DM 3.65x/5.34, mean 4.44x/6.23.
- Table 1, same model, temperature 1, EAGLE-3 [V]: MT-bench 3.07x/4.24, HumanEval 4.13x/5.82, GSM8K 3.32x/4.59, Alpaca 3.90x/5.56, CNN/DM 2.99x/4.39, mean 3.45x/4.92. (Baseten says T=1 costs ~15-25% of speedup [2nd].)
- Tree vs chain [V], Appendix A: EAGLE-2/3 main numbers use TREES: 60 total draft tokens for 7B/8B, depth 6 (EAGLE-2), depth 8 (EAGLE-3), 10 nodes expanded. Chain drafting appears only in serving experiments: chain length 3 in SGLang (Sec 4.3), max chain length 2 in vLLM (Sec 4.4). No chain-vs-tree table in text. So the 6.23 tau is a 60-token-tree number on H100-class GPUs, NOT comparable to our depth-2 chain.
- Chain-only reality check [2nd/V mix]: RedHat Qwen3-8B speculator at k=3 chain: tau 2.13-2.48 (see 2).

## 2. EAGLE-3 / drafters for Qwen3 / Qwen3.5 / hybrids

No EAGLE-3 head for Qwen3.5-9B found (search of HF, 2026-11). What exists:

- Qwen3.5-397B-A17B EAGLE3, AQ-MedAI, 0.6B draft [V] https://huggingface.co/AQ-MedAI/Qwen3.5-397B-A17B-eagle3 : avg acceptance length EAGLE3 vs native MTP: MT-bench 3.03 vs 3.28, GSM8K 3.40 vs 3.54, Math500 3.53 vs 3.66, HumanEval 3.47 vs 3.62, MMStar 2.67 vs 3.21, CEval 1.77 vs 2.34. MTP wins acceptance on every task; throughput roughly a wash (MT-bench 224.09 vs 224.92 tok/s; MMStar MTP +10.4%). Card blames limited training scale. Needs SGLang PR #25408, SGLANG_ENABLE_SPEC_V2=1, --mamba-scheduler-strategy extra_buffer. Data/GPU-hours not stated.
- Qwen3.5 MoE (a1-agents) EAGLE3 [V] https://huggingface.co/Ichigec/a1-agents-eagle3-speculator : 5,000 ShareGPT samples, 1 layer, draft vocab 8,192, DGX Spark GB10 128GB (hours not stated). Per-position accuracy 71.4% / 42.7% / 25.7%, mean accept length 2.02, 1.34x throughput. (Note: 5K samples gives about our existing MTP numbers: a hint that a small-data head does not beat the shipped MTP.)
- Qwen3.6-27B (hybrid GDN) EAGLE-3, Ex0bit [V] https://huggingface.co/Ex0bit/Qwen3.6-27B-PRISM-EAGLE3 : trained with NVIDIA TensorRT Model-Optimizer on self-distilled completions (REAP + UltraChat + tulu-3, agentic/long-context oversampled); hardware/hours not stated. ~0.6B, chain accept length ~2.4 (PRISM-PRO) / 2.2 (stock); tree top-k 4 accept ~3.35 but "throughput-neutral on this hybrid GatedDeltaNet target" because tree build and recurrent-verify cost cancel gain. SGLang 183 vs 93 tok/s = 1.97x (B-class GPU, bf16). Needs patch for aux-hidden capture on the dense Qwen3.6 class.
- Qwen3-8B EAGLE3, RedHat speculators [V via search snippet + card] https://huggingface.co/RedHatAI/Qwen3-8B-speculator.eagle3 : ShareGPT + UltraChat-200k, thinking off; acceptance length at k=3 chain: HumanEval ~2.39, GSM8K ~2.48, CNN/DM ~2.13; k=7 coding 2.69 [2nd, from search summary]. Hours not found.
- Qwen3-8B EAGLE3, AngelSlim [2nd] https://angelslim.readthedocs.io/zh-cn/stable/performance/speculative_decoding/benchmarks.html : model card avg accept 3.5-3.9 at T=0, 2.8-3.2 at T=1 (tree?); vLLM benchmark page ~2.0 for Qwen3-8B, ~1.7-2.0 for 14B/32B. Numbers inconsistent across pages; training data not stated.
- SpecForge (arXiv 2603.18567, https://arxiv.org/html/2603.18567) [V]: Table 1 training step times on 8xH200 seq 4096 (Llama3.1-8B 2.07 s/step); SpecBundle trained on PerfectBlend (~1.4M conversations); Llama-3.1-8B Math500 accept 2.82 (ShareGPT+UltraChat) vs 3.48 (PerfectBlend), a 1.17x gain from data. Qwen3-30B-A3B speedup 1.55x MT-bench / 2.29x LiveCodeBench (Table 2-3, SGLang). No Qwen3.5 / hybrid content; no GPU-hours.
- Hybrid problems [V]: TreeWY arXiv 2608.20961 https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20961 : verifying a draft batch with GDN layers means snapshotting the recurrent state at every draft position; snapshots cannot be shared across tree branches, so wide trees are memory-infeasible (tested on Qwen3.5-35B/397B; no magnitudes in abstract). Pointers (not read): SGLang issue #28511 ReplaySSM, vLLM RFC #47572 / PR #47576 [2nd]. Feature extraction on 3 layers from a hybrid model works (Qwen3.6-27B head above), but serving stacks needed patches.
- Training-cost anchors: vLLM speculators docs [V] https://docs.vllm.ai/projects/speculators/en/latest/user_guide/tutorials/train/ : 5K ShareGPT samples gives "limited" models; production needs "significantly more"; Baseten [V] https://www.baseten.co/blog/how-to-train-custom-eagle-3-heads-for-speculative-decoding/ : 200-300K samples for generic models <=20B, ~100K for narrow domain, 1-2K tokens/sample, TTT length 7-9, train acc should reach 70-80%, regenerate with target. GPU-hours: not published by any source I found.

## 3. Fine-tuning the native MTP head

- FastMTP, arXiv 2509.18362 (Tencent) https://arxiv.org/html/2509.18362v1 [V]. Base MiMo-7B-RL (dense, 36 layers, 1 MTP layer, 152K vocab). One shared-weight MTP head applied recursively; only head trained (210.8M params). 389.4K SELF-DISTILLED samples (target-generated, T=0.6; Sec 2.4, App A). 3 epochs, LR 5e-5, batch 64, under 1 day on a single H20 server (Sec 3.1). Loss weights beta=0.6, K=3 (Eq 1-2).
  - Per-position acceptance: vanilla ~70% / ~10% / ~0% at k=1/2/3; fine-tuned ~80% / 56% / 36% (Sec 3.4, Fig 4a; intro quotes 81/56/36 vs 70/11/2).
  - Speedup (K=3): vanilla MTP 1.21x; fixed-data FT 1.67x; self-distilled FT 1.81x; + 32K frequency-ranked vocab 2.03x (Sec 3.4, Table 1-2); mean tau ~2.66. Best K=3.
  - Relevance: our 2nd-draft acceptance (0.54) is already near FastMTP's post-FT level (0.56); the headroom they found came from a poor vanilla head at position 2+, which Qwen3.5's MTP does not share. Gain for us would mostly be at long context and deeper steps, unproven.
- vLLM speculators MTP finetune [V] https://docs.vllm.ai/projects/speculators/en/latest/user_guide/algorithms/mtp/ and tutorial (train page): supports Qwen3-Next and Qwen3.5 (Qwen/Qwen3.5-9B is the tutorial's example), freezes embed_tokens + lm_head, captures only the final-layer hidden states (--target-layer-ids 32), 3 epochs, beta 0.6 weights ~[0.51,0.31,0.18], full verifier vocab, stitch weights back. No before/after numbers published on those pages.
- NVIDIA NeMo AutoModel MTP guide [V] https://docs.nvidia.com/nemo/automodel/recipes-e2e-examples/mtp-speculative-decoding : lists native-MTP families incl. Qwen3.5 (dense+MoE), DeepSeek-V4, GLM-5.2; example 1 GPU; defines tau_sim metric; no numbers.
- arXiv 2606.12370 (MTP in RL) https://arxiv.org/html/2606.12370 [V]: Qwen3.5-35A3B, 5 MTP steps trained with backbone frozen, Megatron, one epoch, LR 3.5e-5. Acceptance (gamma=3, Table 2) CE baseline Math 75.0 / Code 71.3 / SWE 75.1 / Agent 90.3 / MTBench 65.3; switching loss to end-to-end TV gives +3.0/+3.3/+8.0/+6.7/+2.3 points. Cross-entropy training makes step-1 acceptance DECLINE (Fig 2). Lesson: acceptance-aware (TV) loss beats CE by 2-8 points; CE-only MTP fine-tune can hurt step 1. GPU-hours not reported.
- Community "TempMTP"/"911MTP" Qwen checkpoints exist on HF [2nd]; no verified end-to-end numbers.

## 4. Training feasibility on Apple Silicon

- No MLX or MPS recipe for training EAGLE/Medusa/MTP heads found [searches 2026-11]. Existing MLX work is inference only: mlx-lm discussion #890 https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx-lm/discussions/890 [V].
- Inference datapoint [V], same discussion: Llama-3.1-8B-4bit + pretrained EAGLE-3 (yuhuili), M3 Ultra 96GB, chain only: acceptance 0.34, ~1.34 tokens/step, 118.9 vs 113.7 tok/s = 1.05x (4-bit draft; fp16 draft 0.94x). Per-step budget: draft head ~0.4 ms (4-bit), extra verify token ~1 ms, sync ~0.5 ms vs 8.8 ms/token baseline. Author: tree attention blocked by single-offset KV cache; LLM-written, unverified.
- Memory sizing [CALC]:
  - Hidden cache: 3 layers x 4096 x 2 B (bf16) = 24,576 B = 24 KB/token. 1M tokens = 24.6 GB; 10M = 246 GB; 100M = 2.46 TB. Add 1 layer of next-token labels (4 B/token, negligible). Compare vLLM speculators estimate for Llama-8B [V]: 268 MB per 8192-token sample with its stated formula (32 layers' worth? formula avg_seq_len x num_layers x hidden x bytes), i.e. caching all layers is 10x worse than 3 layers; cache only 3.
  - Qwen3.5 full-attn layers = 8 of 32; GDN layers have no KV, so capturing hidden states for MTP/EAGLE is plain residual-stream reads.
  - 4-bit 9B target weights ~5-5.5 GB resident only if generating online; with a pre-cached dataset the target need not be loaded during training. Trainable layer: 1 decoder layer at 4096 hidden ~ 0.2-0.4B params plus FC; bf16 weights + Adam fp32 states ~ 4-6 GB. Fits 24 GB if target not co-resident.
  - 248k vocab logits [CALC]: per token fp32 = 248,320 x 4 = ~1 MB; a 4096-token sequence = ~4 GB (plus equal for grad), per TTT step. Needs chunked CE (Nebius streaming CE [V]: 128k-vocab fp32 chunk 1024 logits+grad ~0.97 GiB, throughput 6,100 -> 4,450 tok/s/GPU at chunk 1024 vs baseline) or a reduced draft vocab (EAGLE-3 uses 32k; FastMTP 32K/16K frequency-pruned gave +16% throughput, Table 2). lm_head frozen and shared; 248k x 4096 bf16 = ~2 GB.
  - Regeneration cost: generating 200K x ~1.5K tokens = 300M tokens with the target. At ~30 tok/s single-stream on M5 (dflash-mlx baseline for 9B ~30.5 tok/s [V]) that is ~115 days; batched MLX generation might reach several hundred tok/s, still weeks. Even 20K samples (30M tokens) is days. This is the dominant feasibility cost, not training. [CALC]
  - Training throughput on M-series: no published numbers for draft-layer training. Reference for GPU: SpecForge Llama-8B 2.07 s/step on 8xH200 at seq 4096 [V]; scaling to an M5 (a few percent of an H200's flops/bandwidth) is unmeasured: treat as unknown.
- Nebius [V] https://nebius.com/blog/posts/training-speculative-decoders : dense logits for Llama-8B, batch 16 x 32k, vocab 128k fp32 = ~250 GiB (501 GiB with grad); this is why chunked loss matters.

## 5. Apple Silicon / MLX results for EAGLE-style, DFlash, MTP

- dflash-mlx (bstnxbt) https://github.com/bstnxbt/dflash-mlx [V]: Apple M5 Max 64GB, MLX 0.31.1, vs stock mlx_lm baseline. Qwen3.5-9B (quant unstated): baseline 30.95 tok/s -> 135.34 (4.37x, accept 89.55%) at 1k ctx; 3.65x at 2k; 3.06x at 4k (accept 88.31%); 2.22x at 8k (66.94 tok/s, accept 86.67%). No 16k/32k row for 9B. Qwen3.5-27B-4bit 2.37x -> 1.34x from 1k to 8k. Hybrid GDN handled with "tape-replay rollback" (custom Metal kernel replays only accepted steps; no full-state snapshot). No MTP comparison on that page.
- z-lab/Qwen3.5-9B-DFlash (1B draft, 40k seq training, sliding window) https://huggingface.co/z-lab/Qwen3.5-9B-DFlash [V]: B200, concurrency 1, accept length 5.4-5.8 (block 8), 6.9-7.9 (block 16; MT-bench 5.7), speedup up to 5.01x (math500, block 16); training data/compute not stated. Acceptance length here is ~2.5-3x our MTP's 2.1.
- DFlash vs EAGLE-3 vs MTP [V] https://www.lmsys.org/blog/2026-06-15-next-generation-speculative-decoding-dflash-v2 : Qwen3-4B 5-layer drafters, MT-bench EAGLE-3 3.1/1.4x vs DFlash 3.0/2.2x; GSM8K 4.2/2.1x vs 4.2/3.3x. Qwen3.5-397B: DFlash >4.3x baseline and 1.5x native MTP on HumanEval at conc 1 (MTP 7 steps, DFlash block 16, 8xB200).
- MTP on Apple Silicon, mlx-optiq blog https://mlx-optiq.pages.dev/blog/mtp-on-apple-silicon [V]: M4 Pro 24GB, Qwen3.5-9B baseline 19.5 tok/s greedy; MTP speedup K=1 1.28x, K=2 1.19x, K=3 0.89x, K=4 0.74x (table elsewhere says 1.32x at K=1). Reason: on Metal a K=2 verify costs ~2x a K=1 verify (CUDA ~1.1x). Tokens per verify cost: K=2 full accept 1.50. This directly warns that on Apple Silicon verify width, not just acceptance, caps gains; applies equally to a deeper EAGLE chain or tree.
- MTPLX https://github.com/youssofal/MTPLX [2nd]: ~2.24x at T=0.6 for Qwen3.6-27B on M5 Max. EAGLE-3 on MLX (Llama-8B-4bit): 1.05x (above).

## Go/no-go read (my judgment, not a source claim)

- Published EAGLE-3 heads for Qwen3.5-class models do NOT beat the shipped MTP on acceptance (397B: 3.03 vs 3.28 on MT-bench; MoE 5K-sample head: 2.02). Best hybrid chain result: ~2.2-2.4 accept length at ~0.6B draft with large self-distilled data. Our MTP depth 2 is already ~2.1 per verify.
- Tree drafting, where EAGLE-3's 6.2 tau comes from, is throughput-neutral on GDN hybrids (Ex0bit; TreeWY) and not supported by mlx-lm's cache. Apple verify costs scale ~linearly with width.
- Evidence favors DFlash (already ships for Qwen3.5-9B, MLX port 2.2-4.4x at 1-8k ctx) over a custom EAGLE-3 head, and the cheaper experiment is a FastMTP-style fine-tune of the existing MTP head (needs target-generated data and an acceptance-aware loss; no Apple-side training recipe exists).
- Unverified gaps: EAGLE-3 training GPU-hours (nobody published), MLX training throughput, Qwen3.5-9B MTP fine-tune before/after numbers, 16-32k-context DFlash-MLX numbers for 9B.
